Connect your calendar

Paste your calendar's private link. No sign-in or Google Cloud setup needed.

  1. Open Google Calendar settings.
  2. On the left, under Settings for my calendars, click your calendar.
  3. Scroll to Integrate calendar and copy the Secret address in iCal format.
  1. Open Outlook calendar sharing settings.
  2. Under Publish a calendar, pick your calendar and Can view all details, then Publish.
  3. Copy the ICS link.
  1. In the Calendar app (or icloud.com), right-click your calendar → Share Calendar.
  2. Turn on Public Calendar and copy the link (it starts with webcal://).

🔒 Treat this link like a password. It is stored only on this computer (encrypted) and is never shared.
